Strategic intent and operational reality rarely stay aligned by accident. As organizations grow, tools pile up faster than understanding does, and leadership vision ends up depending on a few people to keep explaining it.

We find where strategy and operations have drifted apart and build what connects them again: operating models, workflows, knowledge systems and the software underneath.

## What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| **[Operational Clarity](https://www.bergbacher.com/en/paths/operational-clarity)** | Growth puts pressure on how strategy gets translated into daily work. We map the gaps between strategic intent and operational reality, then design the operating structures, workflows, tooling and dashboards that close them. |
| **[AI-Native Infrastructure](https://www.bergbacher.com/en/paths/ai-native-infrastructure)** | AI is a structural change, not just a new layer of tools. We realign workflows, data, decision rights and human roles so the organization can take on AI coherently. |
| **[Embedded Systems Partnership](https://www.bergbacher.com/en/paths/embedded-systems-partnership)** | We work inside the organization as its translation layer over the long term. Systems architecture, operating-model design, technology decisions and implementation stay with one accountable owner. |

## How we work

- **Architecture.** We diagnose the structure first, then translate strategic intent into operating models, workflows and digital infrastructure.
- **Embedded partnership.** We stay with leadership teams over time when operations, technology and implementation have to stay aligned.
- **The medium comes second.** A workflow, a dashboard, an internal tool or an AI system is only the right answer if it carries the strategy into operations.

## What you'll find here

The repositories in this organization hold the systems we build for that work: web applications, internal tools, knowledge systems and AI-assisted workflows.

Our stack is mainly **TypeScript**, **React** and **Next.js**.
